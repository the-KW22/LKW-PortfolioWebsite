## Issues
### #2 Draggable header ruler — commit 0697674
- Dragging along the ruler scrubs the page live; free movement, no snapping. Pointer x maps to (section index, progress) and then to `trigger.start + p * (trigger.end - trigger.start)` using the ruler's own ScrollTriggers, so it is correct through the pinned Atlas and on case-study rulers.
- 5px threshold separates click from drag; the click after a drag is swallowed; a plain click still smooth-jumps. Triggers are now index-aligned with sections (null for a missing one).
- Bug caught in testing: Haiku's first version did not scroll because pressing on a number link started a native link drag that cancelled the pointer stream; fixed with `onDragStart` preventDefault.
- DRAG cursor label on the ruler; still hidden below 768px; number links unchanged for keyboard.

### #3 Atlas intro misaligned on laptops — commit 5c8dd37
- Cause: intro start zoom fixed at `ZOOM.introFrom = 2.3`; the ~900px title card became ~2060px wide and was cropped on every screen narrower than ~2400px (1366x768: x -352..1708).
- Fix in `AtlasBoard.tsx`: start zoom = max(rest, min(2.3, 0.86·stageW/titleW, 0.8·stageH/titleH)), recomputed on resize. Measured start zoom: 129% at 1366x768, 136% at 1440x900, 145% at 1536x864, 179% at 1896x864, 181% at 1920x1080, unchanged 229% at 2560x1440; title fully visible at all.

### #4 Keep-scrolling cue in the Atlas — commit fb34355
- Bottom-centre "Scroll to continue" mono label with a bobbing lime arrow in the pinned Atlas. Hidden during the zoom-out intro, fades in after it, fades out after ~90% of the pinned section. Pointer-events none, aria-hidden. Not shown in the phone full-screen map, list view, or below 1024px (Haiku's addition). No bob with reduced motion.
- Verified hidden at 5%/40%, visible at 60%, hidden at 95% of the pin at 1366 and 1920; no overlap with the HUD.
- Owner was told it is subtle and can be made bolder; not changed.

### #8 Menu fits one laptop screen — commit 2ea4981
- Cause: link size `clamp(44px, 8vw, 118px)` scaled with width only; 7 lines overflowed short screens.
- Fix: `clamp(36px, min(8vw, 10.5svh), 118px)` in `Menu.tsx`. Measured no menu scroll at 1366x768, 1440x900, 1536x864, 1896x864, 1920x1080, 2560x1440 and 375x812; first link clears the header; Esc and focus return unchanged.
- This commit was made from the app's commit button just before the agent's own commit command.

### #9 Menu links go to the wrong section — commit 0aa3fd4
- Measured at 1440x900 (miss = landing vs section top): ruler numbers were already exact; menu links on the home page did not move the page at all; from a case study, Services missed by -299px and Contact by -1980px.
- Cause 1: `onSectionClick` scrolled while the menu's scroll lock was held; releasing the lock calls Lenis `start()`, whose `reset()` cancelled the scroll. Fix: store the target and scroll right after `unlock("menu")` in `Menu.tsx`.
- Cause 2: arriving at `/#id` from another route jumped before the ScrollTrigger refresh added the pinned Atlas spacer. Fix in `ScrollProvider.tsx`: jump again after the delayed refresh.
- After: 0px miss for every menu link and ruler number, from the top, from inside the Atlas and from a case-study page.

### #10 Screens gallery scroll blocked — commit bc610d9
- Measured: 10 wheel notches over the gallery images moved the page 0px on /projects/coreex and /projects/moonwave (1200px over the heading).
- Cause: the viewport kept `data-lenis-prevent-wheel` in desktop pinned mode; Lenis ignored the wheel there and its CSS `overscroll-behavior: contain` kept it from reaching the page. Plus `scrub: 1` added ~1s lag on top of Lenis.
- Fix in `PinnedGallery.tsx`: remove the attribute in pinned mode only (restored for the phone swipe list), `scrub: true`. After: 1200/1200px on both projects.
