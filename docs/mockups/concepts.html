Six directions,
one to choose

Rough live sketches, not designs. Hover each preview to play with it. Judge the idea and the feeling, not the polish. You can also mix two.

01 / PREMIUM, CALM

Monolith the object

One dark sculptural slab floats in a void. Light sweeps across it as you move. Each project is shown inside or reflected on it, like an exhibit in a gallery.

WHY YOU
Quiet confidence. Says "I build things that feel expensive."
PRELOADER
The slab rises out of darkness while light fills it.
SOUND
Low room tone, soft glassy hover tones.
EASTER EGG
Drag to spin the slab and find your signature on its back.
02 / CALM, ORGANIC

Ink the fluid

A violet fluid simulation reacts to the cursor. Your words and projects surface through the ink, then dissolve back. Every page transition is a wave of ink.

WHY YOU
Shows real WebGL skill (fluid sim) with a soft, artistic feel.
PRELOADER
One drop falls, spreads, and becomes the page.
SOUND
Liquid swells tied to cursor speed.
EASTER EGG
Hold the mouse to "write" with ink; it saves your doodle.
03 / BOLD, EXPLORATIVE

Atlas the canvas

The home page is an infinite canvas you drag around, like a Figma board of your work: projects, sketches, code snippets, notes. Zoom into any project to open it.

WHY YOU
Feels like your real studio desk. Very memorable, easy to explore.
PRELOADER
Items fly in and pin themselves to the board.
SOUND
Paper and pin clicks, soft drag whoosh.
EASTER EGG
Hidden items far off the board (old ver 1.0 sketches).
04 / BOLD, EXPERIMENTAL

Kinetic Type the words

Giant typography is the 3D world. You fly through tunnels and walls of words; letters break apart and rebuild into the next section's headline.

WHY YOU
Loud, editorial, award-site energy. No illustrations needed.
PRELOADER
A counter 000 to 100 made of huge digits you fall into.
SOUND
Deep whooshes as you pass each word layer.
EASTER EGG
Type any word on your keyboard and it joins the tunnel.
05 / PREMIUM, BOLD

One System the particles

Everything on the site is drawn by one particle system. It forms your name, then each project's shape, then the contact form. "One system, many forms" is the full-stack story.

WHY YOU
A clear metaphor for full-stack: one engineer, every layer.
PRELOADER
Scattered dust gathers into "LKW".
SOUND
Granular shimmer as particles reform.
EASTER EGG
Click and hold to explode everything, release to rebuild.
06 / SHARP, CLEVER

X-ray the lens

The site looks polished and editorial, but your cursor is a lens that shows the engineering underneath: wireframes, grid, code and data. Design on top, engineering below.

WHY YOU
Says "I design it AND I build it" in a single interaction.
PRELOADER
The page loads as wireframe first, then "renders" into the final design.
SOUND
Soft scanner hum under the lens.
EASTER EGG
Press X to turn the whole site into its X-ray view.
Sketches in plain 2D canvas for speed. The real site would use WebGL shaders, custom type and sound. Violet and lime kept from our earlier choice.
